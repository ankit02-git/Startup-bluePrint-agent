MARKET RESEARCH AND COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS GUIDE

=== MARKET SIZING FRAMEWORKS ===

TAM, SAM, SOM Model:
- TAM (Total Addressable Market): The total global demand for your product/service
  Example: Global CRM market TAM = $80B
- SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market): Portion of TAM you can realistically serve
  Example: SMB CRM market in North America = $20B
- SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Realistic capture in 3-5 years
  Example: Target 1% of SAM = $200M

Market Sizing Approaches:
1. Top-down: Start with industry reports, narrow down to your segment
2. Bottom-up: Unit economics × total potential customers
3. Value-based: What customers are currently spending on the problem

=== COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS FRAMEWORKS ===

PORTER'S FIVE FORCES:
1. Threat of new entrants (barriers to entry: IP, capital requirements, brand loyalty)
2. Bargaining power of buyers (switching costs, alternatives available)
3. Bargaining power of suppliers (concentration, uniqueness of inputs)
4. Threat of substitutes (alternative solutions to the same problem)
5. Industry rivalry (number of competitors, growth rate, differentiation)

SWOT ANALYSIS COMPONENTS:
- Strengths: Unique technology, cost advantage, strong team, IP
- Weaknesses: Limited funding, small team, no brand recognition
- Opportunities: Emerging market, regulatory change, technology shift
- Threats: Established competitors, market saturation, regulatory risk

COMPETITOR MAPPING DIMENSIONS:
- Price vs. Quality matrix
- Feature completeness vs. Ease of use
- Enterprise vs. SMB vs. Consumer focus
- Geographic coverage

=== CUSTOMER RESEARCH METHODS ===

Qualitative:
- Customer discovery interviews (aim for 50+ interviews)
- Focus groups (8-10 participants)
- Ethnographic observation
- User journey mapping

Quantitative:
- Surveys (minimum 200 responses for statistical significance)
- A/B testing
- Cohort analysis
- NPS (Net Promoter Score) tracking

Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Framework:
- Functional job: What task needs completing?
- Emotional job: How does the customer want to feel?
- Social job: How does the customer want to be perceived?

=== MARKET ENTRY STRATEGIES ===

Blue Ocean Strategy: Create uncontested market space
Niche Domination: Own a specific vertical before expanding
Disruptive Innovation: Target overlooked segments with simpler, cheaper solutions
Platform Strategy: Build network effects through ecosystem thinking
