STARTUP ECOSYSTEM, INCUBATORS, AND GOVERNMENT SCHEMES

=== INDIA STARTUP ECOSYSTEM ===

GOVERNMENT INITIATIVES:
1. Startup India (startupindia.gov.in)
   - DPIIT Recognition: Tax exemptions (3 years), simplified winding-up
   - IPR benefits: 80% rebate on patent filing fees
   - Fund of Funds: INR 10,000 crore corpus managed by SIDBI
   - Seed Fund Scheme: Up to INR 50 lakhs for early-stage startups
   - Startup India Hub: Mentorship and networking platform

2. Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)
   - Atal Incubation Centres (AICs): INR 10 crore grant for setting up incubators
   - Atal Tinkering Labs: For school-level innovation
   - ARISE (Atal Research and Innovation for Science and Excellence)

3. National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations (NIDHI)
   - PRAYAS: Grants up to INR 10 lakhs for prototyping
   - Technology Business Incubators (TBI): Hosted at NITs, IITs, IIMs
   - SEED SUPPORT SYSTEM: INR 50 lakhs per startup

4. MSME Ministry Programs
   - Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)
   - Technology Centre Systems Programme
   - Udyam Registration benefits

=== INDIA TOP STARTUP HUBS ===

BENGALURU:
- India's Silicon Valley
- Top VCs: Sequoia India, Accel India, Lightspeed India
- Top accelerators: NASSCOM 10K, Target Accelerator, 91springboard
- Key sectors: SaaS, fintech, healthtech, deep-tech

MUMBAI:
- Financial capital, strong fintech ecosystem
- Key VCs: Kalaari Capital, Blume Ventures, Matrix Partners India
- Strong: Fintech, media-tech, e-commerce

DELHI-NCR (NOIDA, GURUGRAM):
- Large B2B and enterprise ecosystem
- Key: Edtech (BYJU's, Unacademy HQ), fintech, logistics-tech
- Government proximity advantage

HYDERABAD:
- Government support (T-Hub, TASK)
- Strong: IT services, healthtech, agritech
- T-Hub: Asia's largest innovation hub

CHENNAI:
- Manufacturing and deep-tech focus
- Automotive and hardware startups
- Strong engineering talent pool

PUNE:
- Growing SaaS ecosystem
- Proximity to Mumbai capital
- Automotive and manufacturing tech
