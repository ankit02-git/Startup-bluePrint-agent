STARTUP FUNDING GUIDE - COMPREHENSIVE REFERENCE

=== FUNDING STAGES ===

1. PRE-SEED FUNDING ($10K - $250K)
   - Bootstrapping: Using personal savings, credit cards, or loans from family/friends
   - Angel investors: High net-worth individuals investing $25K-$500K in early-stage ideas
   - Startup competitions: Prizes ranging from $10K to $250K (e.g., TechCrunch Disrupt, Y Combinator Startup School)
   - Government grants: SBIR, STTR grants (US), Startup India Seed Fund (India)
   - Typical use: Prototype development, market validation, MVP creation

2. SEED FUNDING ($500K - $2M)
   - Seed-stage VCs: Firms like Sequoia Seed, First Round Capital, Initialized Capital
   - Angel syndicates: AngelList syndicates, SeedInvest, Republic
   - Accelerators: Y Combinator ($500K for 7%), Techstars ($120K for 6%), 500 Startups
   - Typical use: Product launch, initial hiring, early customer acquisition

3. SERIES A ($2M - $15M)
   - Lead VC firms: Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Benchmark, Accel
   - Typical metrics required: $1M ARR, strong product-market fit, 3x YoY growth
   - Average dilution: 20-30%
   - Typical use: Scale marketing, expand team, enter new markets

4. SERIES B ($15M - $50M)
   - Larger VC firms and growth equity investors
   - Required: Proven business model, $5M+ ARR, clear path to profitability
   - Typical use: Geographic expansion, acquisitions, aggressive hiring

5. SERIES C+ ($50M+)
   - Private equity, hedge funds, late-stage VCs
   - Pre-IPO growth capital
   - Typical use: IPO preparation, international expansion, market dominance

=== GOVERNMENT FUNDING SCHEMES ===

INDIA:
- Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS): Up to INR 50 lakhs for proof-of-concept
- Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS): INR 10,000 crore corpus managed by SIDBI
- Atal Innovation Mission (AIM): Grants for deep-tech startups
- DPIIT Recognition: Tax exemptions, easier patent filing, access to government tenders
- Mudra Loans: Up to INR 10 lakhs for micro-enterprises (Shishu, Kishore, Tarun)
- Stand-Up India: Loans between INR 10 lakhs to INR 1 crore for SC/ST/women entrepreneurs

US:
- SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research): Phase I up to $275K, Phase II up to $1.83M
- STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer): Similar to SBIR with university partnerships
- SBA 7(a) Loans: Up to $5M for eligible small businesses
- USDA Rural Business Grants: For rural-area startups

EUROPE:
- Horizon Europe: €95.5 billion research and innovation framework
- EIC Accelerator: Up to €2.5M grant + €15M equity
- InnovFin: EIB Group loans and guarantees for innovative companies

=== KEY INCUBATORS AND ACCELERATORS ===

GLOBAL TOP TIER:
- Y Combinator (YC): $500K funding, 3-month program, 7% equity, alumni network of 4000+ companies
- Techstars: $120K for 6% equity, global network, 3-month program
- 500 Startups: $150K for 6% equity, strong in emerging markets
- Seedcamp: Europe-focused, €200K for 8-10% equity

INDIA:
- T-Hub (Hyderabad): India's largest startup incubator, government-backed
- NASSCOM 10,000 Startups: IT-focused, mentorship + funding access
- IIM Ahmedabad CIIE: Strong in social enterprise and deep-tech
- IIT Bombay Society for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (SINE)
- Startup Village (Kerala): Focus on hardware and IoT startups

IBM PROGRAMS:
- IBM Hyper Protect Accelerator: For health and financial services startups
- IBM Alpha Zone Accelerator: Israel-based, global focus
- IBM Ventures: Investment arm for strategic startup partnerships
- IBM SkillsBuild for Entrepreneurs: Free access to IBM Cloud, watsonx credits

=== INVESTOR TYPES AND WHAT THEY LOOK FOR ===

Angel Investors:
- Team quality (60% weight)
- Market size (20% weight)
- Early traction (20% weight)
- Expected return: 10-30x in 5-7 years

Venture Capital:
- Scalable business model
- Large addressable market ($1B+)
- Defensible competitive advantage (moat)
- Strong founding team with domain expertise
- Clear path to exit (IPO or acquisition)
- Target return: 3-10x fund

Corporate Venture Capital (CVC):
- Strategic fit with parent company
- Technology differentiation
- Partnership opportunities
- IBM Ventures, Google Ventures, Intel Capital, Salesforce Ventures

=== FUNDRAISING TIPS ===
- Build a strong data room: pitch deck, financial model, cap table, team bios
- Warm intros outperform cold outreach by 400%
- Valuation benchmarks: Pre-seed 2-5x revenue multiple, Seed 5-10x, Series A 10-20x ARR
- Convertible notes: SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) for early rounds
- Due diligence prep: Clean financials, IP ownership, incorporation documents
