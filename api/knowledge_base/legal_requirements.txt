LEGAL REQUIREMENTS AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE FOR STARTUPS

=== BUSINESS ENTITY TYPES ===

UNITED STATES:
1. Sole Proprietorship
   - Simplest, no formal filing required
   - Personal liability for all debts
   - Not suitable for funded startups

2. LLC (Limited Liability Company)
   - Limited personal liability
   - Pass-through taxation
   - Flexible management structure
   - Cost: $50-500 state filing fee
   - Best for: Small teams, service businesses

3. C-Corporation (C-Corp)
   - Required for VC funding (preferred structure)
   - Double taxation (corporate + dividend)
   - Delaware C-Corp is industry standard (75% of VC-backed startups)
   - Allows multiple stock classes (common + preferred)
   - Delaware filing cost: ~$90 + registered agent fee

4. S-Corporation
   - Pass-through taxation like LLC
   - Limited to 100 shareholders, only US citizens
   - Not suitable for VC investment

INDIA:
1. Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)
   - Most popular for funded startups
   - Minimum 2 directors, 2 shareholders
   - Required documents: MoA, AoA, PAN, TAN
   - Registration cost: INR 7,000-15,000
   - ROC registration with Ministry of Corporate Affairs

2. LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)
   - Good for professional services
   - Lower compliance burden than Pvt Ltd
   - Not eligible for DPIIT Startup India recognition for tax benefits

3. OPC (One Person Company)
   - Single founder company
   - No need for second director
   - Annual turnover cap: INR 2 crore

DPIIT STARTUP RECOGNITION (INDIA):
- Eligibility: Incorporated within last 10 years
- Annual turnover < INR 100 crore in any year
- Working towards innovation/improvement of products/services
- Benefits: 3 years tax holiday, easier compliance, fast-track patent processing

=== INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY PROTECTION ===

PATENTS:
- Utility patent: Protects functional inventions (20-year protection)
- Design patent: Protects ornamental design (15 years in US)
- Provisional patent: 12-month placeholder, $320 USD filing fee (small entity)
- PCT (Patent Cooperation Treaty): File once, protect in 150+ countries
- India patent filing: INR 1,600-4,000 for startup/MSME
- Timeline: 2-4 years for grant
- Important: File BEFORE any public disclosure

TRADEMARKS:
- Protects brand name, logo, tagline
- US filing fee: $250-350 per class
- India filing fee: INR 4,500 per class (startup)
- Common law trademark rights exist from first use
- Register early to prevent squatting

COPYRIGHT:
- Automatic upon creation of original work
- Software, content, artistic works
- US Copyright Office registration: $65
- Strengthens legal standing in infringement cases

TRADE SECRETS:
- NDA agreements with employees, contractors, partners
- Non-compete clauses (enforceability varies by state)
- Documented internal security policies

=== KEY LEGAL DOCUMENTS FOR STARTUPS ===

FOUNDERS:
1. Founders' Agreement
   - Equity split and vesting schedule (4 years, 1-year cliff standard)
   - Roles and responsibilities
   - IP assignment to company
   - Non-compete and non-solicitation
   
2. Share Vesting Agreement
   - Standard: 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
   - Accelerated vesting triggers (acquisition, termination)

EMPLOYEES & CONTRACTORS:
1. Employment Agreement
   - At-will employment (US), notice periods (India: 30-90 days)
   - IP assignment clause (critical)
   - Non-disclosure agreement
   - Compensation and benefits

2. Contractor/Freelancer Agreement
   - Work-for-hire clause
   - IP ownership transfer
   - Payment terms and milestones

INVESTORS:
1. SAFE Note (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)
   - YC-standard early stage investment instrument
   - No interest, no maturity date, converts at next priced round
   - Common terms: Valuation cap ($1-10M) and/or discount (15-25%)

2. Convertible Note
   - Debt instrument converting to equity
   - Interest rate (typically 4-8% per year)
   - Maturity date (12-24 months)
   - Conversion discount (15-25%)

3. Term Sheet
   - Non-binding document outlining key investment terms
   - Pre-money valuation, investment amount, equity %
   - Board composition, pro-rata rights, information rights

4. SHA (Shareholders' Agreement)
   - Tag-along, drag-along rights
   - Anti-dilution provisions (weighted average vs. full ratchet)
   - Right of first refusal (ROFR)
   - Voting rights and board composition

CUSTOMER/VENDOR:
1. Terms of Service & Privacy Policy (mandatory for all digital products)
   - GDPR compliance (EU users): Data processing agreements, consent mechanisms
   - CCPA compliance (California users)
   - India IT Act and PDPB compliance

2. Master Service Agreement (MSA) for B2B
3. SLA (Service Level Agreement) for SaaS products

=== REGULATORY COMPLIANCE BY INDUSTRY ===

FINTECH:
- US: SEC registration (if securities), FinCEN (money transmission), CFPB
- India: RBI regulations, SEBI (if investment), IRDAI (if insurance)
- PCI DSS compliance for payment card data
- KYC/AML requirements

HEALTHTECH:
- US: FDA approval (medical devices), HIPAA (health data privacy)
- India: CDSCO registration, Clinical Establishment Act
- ISO 13485 (medical device quality management)

EDTECH:
- FERPA compliance (US, student data)
- India: UGC guidelines for online courses
- COPPA (children under 13, US)

FOODTECH:
- FSSAI license (India), FDA registration (US)
- Food safety certifications
- Packaging and labeling regulations

=== COMPLIANCE CALENDAR FOR INDIAN STARTUPS ===
- GST Registration: Mandatory if turnover > INR 20 lakhs (services)
- GST Return Filing: Monthly/Quarterly (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B)
- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source): Monthly payment, quarterly returns
- Annual ROC Filing: Form AOC-4 (financial statements), MGT-7 (annual return)
- Income Tax Return: By September 30 (with audit), July 31 (without)
- DPIIT Renewal: Not required (one-time recognition)
- PF/ESI Registration: When employee count exceeds 20 (PF) or 10 (ESI)
